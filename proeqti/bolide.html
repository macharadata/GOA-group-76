LE PUR SANG DES AUTOMOBILES

EXTREMELY LIGHTWEIGHT.
HIGH STRENGTH.

Liberating the car of all baggage, the newly-shaped, lightweight carbon monocoque — which had to be constructed around the incredibly compact drive-train — bestows full aerodynamic effectiveness all over.

PHENOMENAL DOWNFORCE

An ultra-light vehicle mastering extreme cornering speeds, yet manageable for all drivers, demands harnessing nearly three tons of downforce — a powerful testament to groundbreaking aerodynamics, uncompromising design, and low center of gravity.

MASTERIN THE AIR

A more prominent front diffuser, along with air curtains and a carefully designed underfloor, create a suction effect while minimizing drag as much as possible.

BESPOKE EMOTIONS

Depending on the chassis setting, drivers can adapt the airflow and aero-balance of their vehicles — granting owners access to unparalleled motorsport performance levels through the highly focused aero package.
